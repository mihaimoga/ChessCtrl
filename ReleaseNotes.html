History

  • Version 1.01 (May 17th, 2026): The first official release of the ChessCtrl demo application marks an important milestone in the development of the project. This initial version showcases the core functionality of ChessCtrl, providing users with a working demonstration of its primary features, user interface, and overall design concept. It represents the transition from prototype to a more polished and structured application, offering a solid foundation for future enhancements, optimizations, and expanded capabilities.
